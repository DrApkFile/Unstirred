## What one proof costs

The three tests in `tests/prove.rs` share one proof, because a real proof is the expensive part:
60.21 s for the file, including the harness wallet, Halo2 key generation on first use, proving,
verifying, signing and the three checks. Key generation is paid once per process.

## What the step added

1. `unstirred-core/src/package.rs`: the proof package of PRD section 3.4, with the delegation
   instance as fourteen 32-byte hex fields. Strict on read: an unknown field, a missing field or a
   wrong version is `PROOF_INVALID`.
2. `unstirred-prover/src/identity.rs`: the age-encrypted mnemonic in the wallet's `keys.toml` is
   decrypted with the holder's identity file, stretched to a seed, and turned into the account's
   Ironwood spending key. The mnemonic and the seed live in zeroizing buffers.
3. `unstirred-prover/src/spend_authority.rs`: two checks before anything is signed. The seed's
   ZIP 32 fingerprint must be the one the account was derived from, and the full viewing key the
   spending key produces must be the account's recorded Ironwood viewing key. A mismatch stops the
   prover; no other derivation is tried.
4. `unstirred-prover/src/bundle.rs`: the circuit's note inputs, each with its witness at h0 and its
   non-membership proof at h1, every proof checked against the pinned h1 root and against the
   note's own nullifier before the builder sees it.
5. `unstirred-prover/src/prove.rs`: `build_delegation_bundle`, `create_delegation_proof`,
   `verify_delegation_proof` here before anything is written, a fresh 32-byte session key, the
   binding message of PRD section 3.6 signed under the alpha-randomized spend key, and that
   signature verified under rk rebuilt from the instance. A package whose own proof or signature
   does not verify is never emitted.
6. `unstirred prove` in the CLI, and `PirNullifierOracle`, which answers both the selected notes
   and the delegation builder's padding slots over one connection.
